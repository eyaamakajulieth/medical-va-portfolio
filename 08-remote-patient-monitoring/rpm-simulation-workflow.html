RPM Review & Escalation Workflow

From incoming reading to documented action

01 Receive

Home reading enters the monitoring workflow.

02 Review

Confirm patient, measurement, date/time and reading.

03 Classify

Apply the clinic’s established monitoring/status protocol.

04 Document

Record the reading and action taken.

05 Route

Send abnormal/critical results to the appropriate clinical team member.

06 Follow Up

Track outstanding provider or patient follow-up.

Example Reading Actions

Demonstrating different RPM workflow outcomes

NORMAL

Example: 118/78 mmHg, pulse 72 bpm
Action: Reviewed and documented in the EHR.

ABNORMAL

Example: 148/92 mmHg
Action: Reviewed and routed to provider per monitoring protocol.

CRITICAL

Example: 168/120 mmHg, pulse 120 bpm
Action: Escalated to clinical team per protocol.

LOW READING

Example: low glucose reading
Action: Reading documented and routed for prompt clinical review according to the established monitoring protocol.

Simulation values are illustrative. Real-world alert thresholds and escalation procedures are determined by the practice’s clinical protocols and patient care plan.

Medical VA Responsibilities

Administrative and operational support within the RPM program

What I Support

  • Review incoming RPM readings.
  • Verify patient and reading details.
  • Document readings and workflow actions.
  • Identify protocol-based alerts for routing.
  • Route abnormal or critical readings to the appropriate clinical team member.
  • Track follow-up requirements and outstanding actions.

Workflow & Documentation

  • Maintain organized RPM records.
  • Support patient outreach when assigned by the care team.
  • Document patient-reported symptoms or communication.
  • Monitor missing or overdue readings.
  • Maintain clear documentation and escalation trails.
  • Protect patient information and follow clinic procedures.
