Project 07 • Medical Virtual Assistant

Pharmacy & Medication Management

Simulated Training Project • Mock Patient

Prescription processing and pharmacy workflow simulation using OptiMantra, demonstrating patient selection, e-prescribing, medication entry, diagnosis and pharmacy selection, review, approval, routing, and documentation.

Project Overview

This simulated Medical Virtual Assistant project demonstrates the administrative workflow for processing a provider's prescription after a patient consultation, diagnosis, and laboratory examination.

The workflow uses OptiMantra to locate the patient, open the e-prescribing workflow, create the prescription, enter medication instructions, associate the diagnosis, select the pharmacy, review the prescription, support the approval and routing process, and retain the prescription document as workflow evidence.

Pharmacy Workflow

STEP 01

Patient Selection

Search for the mock patient in the OptiMantra patient list and confirm the correct patient record before proceeding.

STEP 02

Open Patient Chart

Open the patient's Actions menu and access the eRx workflow under the Clinical section.

STEP 03

Launch eRx Workflow

Open the patient's e-prescribing screen before creating the new prescription.

STEP 04

Create Prescription

Open Prescription Writer and select New Order to begin entering the provider's prescription.

STEP 05

Enter Medication Details

Enter the medication, directions, quantity, days supply, refills, and substitution preference.

STEP 06

Add Diagnosis & Pharmacy

Associate the prescription with the documented diagnosis and select the patient's retail pharmacy.

STEP 07

Review Prescription Settings

Review the effective date and prescription controls before moving forward with the workflow.

STEP 08

Review & Approve

Review the prescription in the medication list and capture the approval status.

STEP 09

Complete & Document

Complete the prescription workflow and retain the available routing and documentation information.

STEP 10

Retain Prescription Evidence

Retain the prescription document as evidence of the completed simulated pharmacy workflow.
