About Me

Healthcare is more than patient care. It is also about keeping the work behind that care accurate, organized, and moving.

I’m a Medical Radiographer and Medical Virtual Assistant focused on supporting healthcare teams with the administrative and clinical workflows that keep patient care running smoothly.

I support patient onboarding and scheduling, insurance verification, prior authorization, medical billing and claims, lab order management, pharmacy and medication workflows, medical scribing, remote patient monitoring, and patient follow-up.

My portfolio demonstrates practical simulated healthcare workflows using healthcare platforms and tools, with a focus on accuracy, organization, documentation, timely follow-up, and attention to detail.

You focus on your patients. I’ll help keep the work behind the scenes organized, accurate, and moving forward.

Looking for dependable healthcare support? Let’s work together.
