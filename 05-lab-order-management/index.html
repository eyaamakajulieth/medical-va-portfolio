Lab Order Management

Simulated Training Project • Mock Patient

Lab order management workflow using OptiMantra, demonstrating patient chart review, provider request processing, lab requisition generation, documentation, patient communication, and follow-up scheduling.

Project Overview

This simulated Medical Virtual Assistant project demonstrates how a lab order can be managed inside OptiMantra after a provider's consultation.

The workflow covers locating the correct patient, reviewing the provider's lab request, creating the order, generating the lab requisition, documenting the task, communicating with the patient, and securing a follow-up appointment.

Lab Order Workflow

STEP 01

Locate the Patient

Search for the patient in OptiMantra and verify the correct chart using identifying information such as date of birth.

STEP 02

Open the Patient Chart

Review the patient's chart and confirm the demographic information and relevant diagnoses before proceeding.

STEP 03

Review Provider's Request

Review the provider's requested laboratory tests, clinical reason, and instructions before entering the order.

STEP 04

Create the Lab Order

Enter the requested laboratory order in the patient's OptiMantra chart and ensure the required information is accurately documented.

STEP 05

Generate the Requisition

Generate the laboratory requisition and save the document in the patient's document history for processing.

STEP 06

Document the Workflow

Confirm that the order and supporting documentation have been saved correctly in the patient's chart.

STEP 07

Communicate With the Patient

Send a privacy-conscious message reminding the patient to complete the blood draw and contact the laboratory to book.

STEP 08

Schedule Follow-Up

Schedule a test-result follow-up appointment and confirm that the appointment appears on the practice calendar.
