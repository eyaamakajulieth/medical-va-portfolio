Project Overview

This project demonstrates a simulated medical billing and claims workflow using mock patient information. The workflow covers direct insurance claim submission for in-network billing and out-of-network / out-of-pocket billing, including the use of a CMS-1500 form and superbill.

Two Primary Billing & Claim Submission Methods

The simulation covers two primary approaches to billing and claim submission.

1

Direct Insurance Claim Submission

In-Network Billing

The provider submits the claim directly to the payer through:

  • 837P — Electronic professional claim submitted through a clearinghouse.
  • CMS-1500 — Paper professional claim form when paper submission is permitted.
2

Out-of-Network / Out-of-Pocket Billing

Used when the out-of-network provider does not bill the patient's insurance directly.

  • Patient pays the provider out of pocket.
  • The practice generates a superbill containing services, codes, charges, and provider information.
  • The patient submits the superbill to insurance for possible reimbursement based on out-of-network benefits.
