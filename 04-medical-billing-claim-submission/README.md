# Medical Billing & Claim Submission

## Project Overview

Simulated Medical Virtual Assistant training project demonstrating key medical billing and claim submission workflows, including electronic billing, superbill preparation, CMS-1500 claim forms, and patient self-pay/out-of-pocket billing workflows.

## Billing Workflows Demonstrated

### 1. Electronic Billing & Claim Submission
Demonstration of preparing and processing billing information for electronic claim submission.

### 2. Superbill
Demonstration of reviewing and preparing a superbill containing relevant patient, provider, diagnosis, procedure, and billing information.

### 3. CMS-1500 Claim Form
Demonstration of completing and reviewing a CMS-1500 claim form for professional healthcare services.

### 4. Patient Self-Pay / Out-of-Pocket Billing
Demonstration of documenting and managing billing situations where the patient is responsible for payment rather than submitting the charge to insurance.
