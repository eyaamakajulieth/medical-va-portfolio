Project Overview

This project demonstrates a complete patient onboarding and scheduling workflow for a simulated women's health practice.

The workflow focuses on making sure that a new patient is properly registered, receives the necessary onboarding information, gains access to the patient portal, has an appointment scheduled based on provider availability, and receives confirmation and required pre-appointment forms.

The goal is to create an organized experience for both the patient and the healthcare team while ensuring that important administrative steps are completed before the appointment.

Patient Onboarding Workflow

The workflow was structured into six stages, taking the patient from the initial request for an appointment through appointment confirmation and pre-appointment preparation.

STEP 01

Appointment Request

The patient contacts the clinic to request an appointment. The appointment request and patient intent are documented as the starting point of the onboarding process.

STEP 02

Patient Profile Creation

Patient demographic information is captured and the patient profile is created in the appropriate system.

STEP 03

Intake Form & Welcome Message

The required intake information is assigned and a welcome message is sent to the patient.

STEP 04

Patient Portal Support

The patient is supported through the portal experience so they can access their welcome information and complete the required onboarding steps.

STEP 05

Appointment Scheduling

Provider availability is reviewed and the patient's appointment is scheduled based on the available time.

STEP 06

Confirmation & Pre-Appointment Forms

The appointment is confirmed and the patient receives the necessary intake and consent forms before the appointment date.
