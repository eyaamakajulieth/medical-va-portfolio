# Pharmacy & Medication Management

## Project Overview

Simulated Medical Virtual Assistant training project demonstrating pharmacy and medication management workflows using OptiMantra.

The project focused on managing medication-related information within an EHR environment and supporting organized prescription and pharmacy workflows.

## Tool Used

OptiMantra

## Project Type

Simulated Training Project — Mock Patient Data

## Pharmacy & Medication Management Workflows

### 1. Medication Management

- Reviewing and managing patient medication information
- Recording medication-related details in the EHR
- Maintaining organized medication records

### 2. Prescription Management

- Reviewing prescription information
- Documenting prescription details accurately
- Managing relevant medication and prescription information

### 3. Pharmacy Workflow

- Reviewing pharmacy-related information
- Supporting medication and prescription workflows
- Documenting pharmacy-related activities within the EHR

### 4. Medication Documentation

- Maintaining accurate medication records
- Reviewing medication information for completeness
- Organizing medication-related documentation

## Skills Demonstrated

- Pharmacy workflow support
- Medication management
- Prescription documentation
- EHR navigation
- Medication record management
- Attention to detail
- Patient confidentiality
- Healthcare administrative support
